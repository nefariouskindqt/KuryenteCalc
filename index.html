Energy Saving Tips

  • Air Conditioning: Clean filters monthly and set thermostat to 25°C. Inverter ACs can save up to 40% energy.
  • Refrigerator: Keep it away from heat sources. Don't leave the door open for long and avoid putting hot food inside.
  • Lighting: Switch to LED bulbs. They consume 80% less electricity than incandescent bulbs and last longer.
  • Vampire Power: Unplug appliances not in use. Devices on standby mode still consume electricity.

How to Read Your Bill

To manually compute your bill, you only need three numbers from your physical or digital statement. Here is where to find them on a standard PH bill (like Meralco):

YOUR UTILITY COMPANY BILLING STATEMENT

Account No: 1234567890

Name: JUAN DELA CRUZ

Billing Period: Aug 2026

1

METER READING

Previous: 4520
Present: 4770
Actual Consumption: 250 kWh
2

BILL COMPUTATION

Generation Charge ₱7.50 / kWh
Distribution Charge ₱2.50 / kWh
Transmission, Taxes, etc. ₱4.78 / kWh
Effective Rate this Month: ₱14.78 / kWh
1

Present & Previous Readings

This shows what your meter displayed last month versus this month. Subtracting the previous from the present gives you your total kWh consumed.

2

Effective Rate

Your bill is made of Generation (the highest), Transmission, Distribution, and Taxes. Providers usually compute an "Effective Rate" (Total Bill Amount ÷ Total kWh). You can use this rate to estimate future bills.
